Born in Montpellier, artist and author Oriana Valenza has worked in painting, writing, singing and stage choreography. From her beginnings at the opera, through gospel and her trip hop band, she has never given up singing. Her epic voice, set on promoting communication and expression as tools for psychological well-being, offers an inner journey in this project.

Following her rape and her chronic illnesses (epilepsy, endometriosis, fibromyalgia), and a traumatic journey, she shares open reflections in her compositions, to dare to stop hiding.

This project is a challenge for herself, because her generalized anxiety disorder gives her monumental stage fright, BUT she never wants to give up. Her voice will have been her path to resilience.
